Layout and Codenotch motion

  • 5H stays vertical, retaining the approved liquid vessel, colour changes and reset countdown.
  • Weekly becomes horizontal, separating quota capacity from time until reset.
  • Notch glance changes too, a ring for 5H and a small bar for Weekly, while preserving account hover grouping.
  • Motion: Native spring equations, Codenotch response/damping values, 45 ms staggered elements and smooth quota readings.
  • Side controls: Display, Tasks and Settings remain at the top of the notch, even when Piggy is expanded. Settings reveals Quit, Piggy and Sound on hover.
  • Liquid shortcuts: Display and Settings use the same spring-driven gooey SVG separation.
  • Audio mixer: Output and Input channels, device selection and per-app volume, mute and routing use the approved dark compact design. All controls are simulated.
  • Main views: Piggy, Audio, Display and Tasks all expand downward from the same notch shell, with no floating panels or X buttons.
  • Notch interaction: Click the blank top edge or outside to collapse; click the compact body to expand again.
  • Unchanged: Piggy icon, black notch, approved quota visuals, account tabs, top actions and banked resets.
